### B0 conventions

For the side to move, let `dSelf` and `dOther` be shortest **wall-only** goal distances and `wSelf`, `wOther` remaining wall counts. The nonterminal value is `tanh((dOther - dSelf)/6 + (wSelf - wOther)/20)`, in `[-1,1]` from the side-to-move perspective. Pawn prior weight is `max(0.2, 2 + dSelf - dNext)`; ordinary wall weight is `0.5`. Immediate pawn wins get weight `1000`. When the opponent's wall-only distance is one, moves that prevent an immediate legal winning pawn reply get weight `1000`; moves that allow one get `0.01`. Weights are normalized over the authoritative legal-action list. Invalid policy falls back to uniform. Terminal value is `+1` if the side to move won and `-1` if the opponent won (in ordinary terminal positions the turn has switched, so a new winner is `-1` at the child). PUCT selects `Q + 1.5 × P × sqrt(parentVisits + 1)/(edgeVisits + 1)`, with `Q` in the parent perspective; backup flips the sign on **every** pawn or wall edge. Action order is ascending ID, and fixed seed hashing breaks ties. A depth cap/cycle only takes a heuristic leaf; no exact result is claimed. Search strength has not been benchmarked.

### Session undo and recovery

PvP undo remains one ply. In AI mode, an AI-first opening at ply 1 has no preceding human decision, so undo is disabled. The preceding actor is the opposite of the authoritative View's next turn, including terminal Views; this can be reconstructed after replay import. During AI thought immediately after a human move, undo removes that human move. After an AI reply, undo removes the human move and reply, returning to the preceding human decision (human-first ply 2 → 0; AI-first ply 3 → 1). Restored human-first thinking plies 1 and 3 undo to 0 and 2; restored AI-first thinking plies 2 and 4 undo to 1 and 3. A human-winning final move undoes one ply; an AI-winning reply undoes two. Cancelling AI retains the current game and exposes retry or PvP takeover. If the Rust undo call fails after AI invalidation, the session exposes `recoverableError` with retry/PvP takeover instead of remaining in a cancelled AI turn. A valid replay restore creates a new epoch; failed validation leaves the old epoch, revision, position and search coherent. There is no product save/load UI yet.

### Corrective review F1: restored AI undo

Lead's browser repro showed that transient `lastActor = null` after replay restore made a human-first ply 1 undo fail and ply 3 undo to ply 1, leaving an AI turn labelled `humanTurn`. `SessionController.undoTarget` now computes the last actor from the Rust View's `turn`, chooses one ply after a human action or two after an AI reply, and verifies the target exists **before** cancelling AI. The target is therefore reconstructible from replay. After a successful undo, the session only enters `humanTurn` when the resulting View turn matches the human assignment (or the mode is PvP). If the Rust undo call fails after AI invalidation, `recoverableError` exposes retry or PvP takeover without changing the revision or position.

The new browser test uses actual release/development rules Wasm to create and restore standard replays, with a waiting AI test double for response timing and a forced undo fault. It covers human-first pending plies 1→0 and 3→2; AI-first pending plies 2→1 and 4→3; AI-first opening ply 1 with undo disabled; completed AI replies, both human/AI terminal actor interpretations, cancelled thought, PvP one-ply undo, a deliberately late AI result after undo, and an injected undo failure followed by retry/PvP takeover. Position key remains stable after the late result and failed undo. It uses no position injection or TypeScript rules oracle. Targeted browser test passed, then all 18 dev, 18 root-production and 18 `/quoridor/` production tests passed on final executable source. The 15 core, 7 AI, 6 rules-wire and 2 AI-wire tests and generated DTO/fixture checks passed again through `npm run check`; native and search source did not change in this correction.

The B0 heuristic is intentionally small and training-free. Model downloads/training, strength claims, exact endgame solving, tree reuse, GPU inference, broad persistence/recovery UI, real VXGI/TRAA and host Chrome remain outside Phase 3. The accepted Phase 0–2 game/render boundary remains in place.
